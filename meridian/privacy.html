Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026
The short version: Meridian does not collect any personal data. It works fully offline, has no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Meridian app ("the app", "we", "us"), published by CJ Toolseram, handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party data-collection SDKs are used.
  • The app makes no network requests for its content — the reference material is bundled in the app and works entirely offline.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences and any items you save (for example, favorited points or combos, and settings) are stored only on your device. We never receive or have access to this data. If you back up your device, that data may be included in your own private Apple backup, governed by Apple's terms. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Purchases

Optional premium features are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payments and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive your payment details or identity. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregate sales information. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Data sharing & selling

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data, because we do not collect any.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Any data the app stores lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version on this page.
