Frequently asked

Is this medical advice?
No. Meridian is for education and self-care exploration only — it does not diagnose or treat. See a licensed practitioner or physician for medical concerns.
How do I restore a previous purchase?
Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases, or reinstall and tap it there. Your lifetime unlock and subscription are tied to your Apple Account.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open your Apple Account subscriptions (or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device).
Does it work offline?
Yes — all content is bundled in the app and works with no network connection. There are no ads.
Do you collect my data?
No. See the Privacy Policy — the app collects nothing and works fully offline.
