Meridian: Acupressure Atlas

A calm, offline guide to the twelve primary meridians and 361 classical acupressure points of Traditional Chinese Medicine — each point's location, pinyin and Chinese name, and what it is traditionally associated with in TCM. Locations follow the WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations and A Manual of Acupuncture. Includes the Meridian Clock, Today's point, and Study Mode. Works fully offline, with no ads and no data collected.
